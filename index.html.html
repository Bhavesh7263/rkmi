Radhe Krishna Metal Industries
Stockist · Trader · Supplier · Est. 2005

Radhe Krishna Metal Industries is a leading stockist, trader, and supplier of Stainless Steel Pipes, Tubes, and allied products based in Mumbai, Maharashtra. Established in 2005, we have built a strong reputation for quality, reliability, and competitive pricing.

We serve clients across Oil & Gas, Chemical, Pharmaceutical, Construction, and General Engineering sectors. Our large inventory ensures availability and quick turnaround for all standard and special requirements.

Every product comes with complete documentation including Material Test Certificates (MTC), ensuring traceability and compliance with international standards like ASTM, ANSI, and EN.
